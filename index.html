Daechul Ahn

I'm a Ph.D. student at SNU Machine Perception and Reasoning Lab. at Seoul National University advised by Prof. Jonghyun Choi.

My research focuses on building embodied AI agent that perceive, reason, and act like humans, progressing from video-language understanding and multimodal alignment to multi-agent reasoning and real-world robotic manipulation.
